The Company

Miss Star International LLC is a pioneering organization dedicated to celebrating diversity, empowerment, and representation in the beauty pageant industry. Founded and led by Executive Director Maxima Victoria Caram, we provide a global platform that showcases the talent, grace, and achievements of transgender women worldwide.

Our Values

Inclusivity

We believe in creating spaces where every voice is heard and every story matters. Our platform celebrates the diverse experiences and backgrounds of our contestants, fostering a global sisterhood that spans all continents.

Empowerment

Through education, mentorship, and opportunities, we empower transgender women to achieve their full potential and inspire others. Our initiatives extend beyond the pageant runway to create lasting impact and personal growth.

Excellence

We maintain the highest standards in our events and operations, ensuring a professional and memorable experience for all participants. Our commitment to quality has been recognized by organizations and government officials alike.

Innovation

We continuously evolve and adapt, bringing fresh perspectives to the pageant industry while honoring its traditions. Through our consortium model and reality show format, we're redefining what a beauty pageant can achieve.

Our Mission

To create a world where beauty knows no boundaries, where transgender women are celebrated for their authenticity, and where their stories inspire positive change in society. We strive to restore trust, foster transparency, empower national directors, and elevate contestant experiences through innovative formats and global collaboration.

Our Vision

To be the leading platform that transforms the beauty pageant industry by promoting inclusivity, challenging stereotypes, and creating opportunities for transgender women globally. We aim to build a comprehensive ecosystem spanning entertainment, education, and employment opportunities that empower our contestants far beyond the pageant itself.

Legal Foundation

Miss Star International operates under registered trademark No. 4.238.776, legally owned by Victoria Perla Alvarez Caram, filed with the Spanish Patent and Trademark Office. Our business is structured to foster collaboration through our unique consortium model that provides equity opportunities for national directors and key stakeholders.
