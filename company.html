The Company

Miss Star International LLC is a pioneering organization dedicated to celebrating diversity, empowerment, and representation in the beauty pageant industry. Our mission is to provide a global platform that showcases the talent, grace, and achievements of transgender women worldwide.

Our Values

Inclusivity

We believe in creating spaces where every voice is heard and every story matters. Our platform celebrates the diverse experiences and backgrounds of our contestants.

Empowerment

Through education, mentorship, and opportunities, we empower transgender women to achieve their full potential and inspire others.

Excellence

We maintain the highest standards in our events and operations, ensuring a professional and memorable experience for all participants.

Innovation

We continuously evolve and adapt, bringing fresh perspectives to the pageant industry while honoring its traditions.

Our Mission

To create a world where beauty knows no boundaries, where transgender women are celebrated for their authenticity, and where their stories inspire positive change in society.

Our Vision

To be the leading platform that transforms the beauty pageant industry by promoting inclusivity, challenging stereotypes, and creating opportunities for transgender women globally.
