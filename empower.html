Our Programs

The Empower TransNation Network offers a range of programs designed to support individuals at every stage of their journey. From educational workshops to leadership development, our initiatives are tailored to meet the diverse needs of our community.

Leadership Academy

A comprehensive program that equips participants with the skills, knowledge, and confidence to become effective leaders in their communities. Through workshops, mentorship, and hands-on projects, participants develop their unique leadership style and vision.

Mentorship Initiative

Connecting experienced activists and professionals with emerging leaders to provide guidance, support, and inspiration. Our mentorship program creates meaningful relationships that foster personal and professional growth.

Educational Workshops

A series of workshops covering a wide range of topics, from advocacy and public speaking to self-care and community organizing. These interactive sessions provide practical skills and knowledge for effective activism.

Global Exchange Program

Facilitating cross-cultural learning and collaboration through international exchanges, virtual forums, and global projects. This program broadens perspectives and builds a global network of advocates working towards common goals.

The Bangkok Initiative

Miss Beyond the Crown: Bangkok's Legacy of Love and Leadership

In the heart of Bangkok, a powerful movement was born—one that reminds us all that beauty is not only about crowns, but about the courage to uplift others. Inspired by the radiant spirit and activism of our very own Miss Beyond the Crown, a group of former contestants and community leaders came together to form the Miss Beyond the Crown Foundation—a groundbreaking initiative rooted in compassion, solidarity, and purpose.

What started as a gesture of gratitude has blossomed into an annual tradition that touches countless lives. Every year during the dazzling gala of Miss Star Thailand, something extraordinary happens. As the lights shine and the queens grace the stage, the audience—both in the auditorium and across the world online—takes part in something even more meaningful: giving back.

Annual Gala Impact

  • Live donations during show
  • Online platform contributions
  • Phone-in pledges worldwide
  • Direct support to HIV/AIDS Hospital

A Legacy of Purpose

This is more than charity—it is legacy. It is the embodiment of what Miss Star International truly stands for: visibility with purpose, fame with responsibility, and beauty with a mission.

The Miss Beyond the Crown Foundation reminds us that true queens don't just wear crowns—they build bridges, heal hearts, and change futures. Through their leadership, a new standard has been set.
